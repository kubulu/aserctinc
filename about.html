Who We Are

A Different Kind of Real Estate Partner

Founded in 2012, Ascert Inc. is an independently-owned, full-service corporate real estate services company headquartered in Bangalore, India.

We provide value-added, client-centric real estate solutions to owners and occupiers of office, retail, industrial, and multi-residential properties. By combining an integrated team approach with deep expertise across a broad range of professionals nationwide, we align your real estate strategy directly with your primary business objectives.
